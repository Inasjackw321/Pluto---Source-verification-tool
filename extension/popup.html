Warning levels

⚑ State Propaganda Government-controlled outlet with no editorial independence. Content reflects official state narrative. Worst offenders have content hidden by default.
◈ State-Funded Funded by a government but may have some editorial independence. Coverage can reflect the funder's interests on key conflicts.
⚠ Unverified Frequently posts unverified, misleading, or false information — particularly during conflict. Low correction rate.
✕ Conspiracy Regularly promotes conspiracy theories, pseudoscience, or fringe narratives about geopolitical events.
✎ Satire Satirical publication. All content is fictional. Flagged to prevent viral spread of headlines as real news.

Sources & methodology

Accounts are flagged based on: government ownership records, foreign-agent registrations (FARA, EU), broadcast licence revocations (Ofcom), academic research (Oxford Internet Institute, Stanford Internet Observatory), and fact-checking databases (NewsGuard, Bellingcat).
