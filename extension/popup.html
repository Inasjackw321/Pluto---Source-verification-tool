Warning categories

★ State Propaganda Government-controlled, no editorial independence. Worst cases have content blocked.
◉ State-Funded Funded by a government but may retain some editorial independence.
▲ Unverified Frequently posts false or misleading claims during conflicts with low correction rates.
✕ Conspiracy Regularly promotes conspiracy theories or fringe narratives about geopolitical events.
ꙅ Satire Fictional publication flagged to prevent viral spread of headlines as real news.

Methodology & sources

Accounts are flagged based on: foreign-agent registrations (FARA, EU), broadcast licence actions (Ofcom, FCC), academic research (Oxford Internet Institute, Stanford Internet Observatory), and fact-checking databases (Bellingcat, NewsGuard). Hover any badge on Twitter for per-account citations.
