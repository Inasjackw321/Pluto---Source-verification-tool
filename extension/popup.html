Warning categories

★ State-Affiliated Government-controlled with no editorial independence. Worst cases have content blocked.
◉ State-Funded Funded by a government; may retain partial independence but serves state interests.
▲ Bad Source Consistently low accuracy, partisan fringe, or documented misinformation track record.

Methodology & sources

Accounts are flagged based on: foreign-agent registrations (FARA, EU), broadcast licence actions (Ofcom, FCC), academic research (Oxford Internet Institute, Stanford Internet Observatory), and fact-checking databases (Bellingcat, NewsGuard). Hover any badge on Twitter or YouTube for per-account citations.
